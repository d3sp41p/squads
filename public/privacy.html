Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

What we collect

  • Wallet addresses you sign in with, and the time you signed in.
  • Squad listings you publish: name, photo, banner, region, entry PNL, description and Pumpfun invite.
  • Wallet addresses of squad members that a squad creator adds when importing a squad.
  • Trading PNL of signed-in wallets, read from public blockchain data through a third-party data provider (Solana Tracker).
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. We use no tracking or advertising cookies.

Wallet addresses can be personal data in the EU. Everything we show (wallets, PNL, squads) is visible to anyone who visits the site.

Why

To list squads, check who meets a squad's entry PNL, and show squad PNL and the leaderboard. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the service you chose to use.

Who processes it

Hosting by Vercel, database by Supabase, PNL data by Solana Tracker. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

How long

Until the competition ends and for up to 3 months after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Signing out clears your session cookie.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. Squad creators can delete their listing themselves at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
